The power unit makes 750 kW: 1,006 horsepower, and half of it is electric. A 1.6-litre V6 turbo makes 400 kW and the electric motor 350 kW, on 100% sustainable fuel. Every lap it recovers 8.5 MJ to spend again.

It reaches 100 km/h in 2.6 seconds and tops out at 352 km/h. The wheelbase is 3,400 mm, the most the rules allow.
