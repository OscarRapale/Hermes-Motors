The winged H: two uprights, a crossbar, and three feathers at 14 degrees.

Hermes was born at dawn. By midday he had invented the lyre; by evening he had outwitted Apollo. His sandals had wings, and so does our H.

The mark leans forward at 14 degrees, the same angle as the stripes that open the site at 06:00. The feathers are Redline, longest on top. On the drivers' helmets they sit just behind the visor.
