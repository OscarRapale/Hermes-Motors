The car comes apart in five chapters on the home page. Scroll, and every part lifts clear of the floor.

The wings move. The front wing has two moving elements, the rear wing three: Straight mode for low drag, Corner mode for grip.

The halo is grade 5 titanium. It must hold 125 kN from above for five seconds, about 12 tonnes, and 125 kN from the side.

The floor is flat: it replaces the venturi tunnels. The wheels are 18-inch, the tyres 25 mm narrower at the front and 30 mm at the rear.

The car weighs 768 kg, down from 800. Behind the driver sits the reason it moves: 750 kW, half of it electric.
