# Voice

From the brand book (board 01, Platform).

**The idea.** Speed is the message. Hermes was born at dawn. By midday he had invented the lyre. By evening he had outwitted Apollo. A debut team has no history to sell, only a first day. We intend to use all of it.

**Positioning.** For people who love the engineering of speed, Hermes Motors is the Grand Prix newcomer that lets its performance do the talking.

## Personality

| Trait | Means | On the page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Precise | Every number earned. | Figures from facts.md only, with units. No "up to", no "nearly". |
| Quietly audacious | A rookie's nerve, a veteran's calm. | Confident statements, no hype words, no exclamation marks. |
| Swift | Fewer words, faster laps. | Short sentences. Headlines of three lines or fewer. One idea per paragraph. |

## Say / don't say

| Say | Don't say |
| --- | --- |
| 1,006 horsepower. Half of it electric. | Unleash mind-blowing power! |
| Day one. | The most exciting debut in racing history! |
| Beat the messenger. | Think you can beat our test driver?? |
| Be here at 06:00. | Don't miss our epic launch event! |
| The car is revealed at 06:00 at the Proving Ground. | Coming soon to a screen near you. |

## Names and spellings

- **Hermes Motors** (no accent; the fashion house is Hermès, and we are not it). Short form: Hermes.
- **HM‑27**, always with a non-breaking hyphen (U+2011) in running text.
- **Day One, First Light, Before Dawn** — the campaign's three acts, capitalised.
- **the Proving Ground** — the team's track, capitalised, with "the".
- **the messenger** — the test driver, lower case in sentences ("race the messenger"), capitals in Michroma headlines.
- Times: 06:00 (24-hour, leading zero). Lap times: 1:11.906. Gaps: −0.214 (true minus sign U+2212) and +0.152.
- Numbers: thousands separators (1,006; 3,400 mm), a thin non-breaking space is fine before units in Geist Mono.

## Headline pattern

Three short lines in Michroma capitals, the middle line dimmed to 42% ink: "SPEED IS / THE / MESSAGE." · "A MESSAGE / IS ON ITS / WAY." · "EVERY PART / CARRIES THE / MESSAGE." Keep line breaks where the copy deck puts them.

## Microcopy

- Buttons say what happens: "Beat the messenger", "Run it back", "Share your card", "Read the manifesto".
- Locked things say when they open: "Test drive opens 06:00".
- Errors are short and practical: "Your browser can't run the test drive. Here's the messenger's lap instead."
- The fictional-team line is plain, never jokey.
