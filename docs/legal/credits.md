## The helmet (CC BY 4.0, attribution required)

> Driver helmet based on a model by Miopass, CC BY 4.0, modified.

- Author: Miopass ([sketchfab.com/miopass](https://sketchfab.com/miopass))
- Source: <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/f1-helmet-zhouguanyu-24-1124fa1f754645d7b9d54de84009d463>
- Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Changes: every original texture removed; repainted in the Hermes livery (feathers, trim band, spoilers, number); parts regrouped; simplified from 98,724 to 37,991 triangles; rescaled to metres.
- The line names the author and links the source but not the source's title, on purpose: the source replicates a real driver's 2024 helmet. None of that design (paint, logos, text) survives here, CC BY 4.0 doesn't require the title, and a real driver's name on a fictional team's site would suggest a connection that doesn't exist. Keep it that way.

Where the line must appear: every page footer (combined with the car's), `/credits` with the links above, the Drivers OG image, and any share card or press image that shows a helmet.
