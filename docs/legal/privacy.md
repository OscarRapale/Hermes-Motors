**PRIVACY**

Hermes Motors is a fictional team and this site is a portfolio project. It collects as little as a website can.

- **No accounts, no sign-up, no cookies.** There is nothing to log in to.
- **Your browser keeps two small things,** only on your device: whether you are previewing the other side of the clock (cleared when you close the tab), and your best lap and attempt count in the test drive. Clear your site data to remove them.
- **Share links carry your lap.** When you share a result, the link contains your lap and sector times, and nothing else. Anyone with the link can see those times.
- **Analytics:** *[if D6 = none]* We don't run analytics. *[if D6 = Vercel Web Analytics]* We count page views with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you.
- **Hosting:** the site is served by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations, under [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- **Fonts and images** are served from this site; your browser doesn't contact third parties while you browse.
