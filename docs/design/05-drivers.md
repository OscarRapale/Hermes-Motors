# 05 · Drivers

Board 25 (`firstLight` switch shows the night version). Prototype `Site-Drivers.dc.html`. Data `content/drivers.ts` (placeholders, Decision D4).

- **Hero:** eyebrow "DRIVERS · SEASON 2027", H1 56 px three lines, intro on the right (17 px, max 460).
- **Two cards** side by side (680 px tall, radius 28): driver 9 on white, driver 19 on Carbon. Each: a large outline number top right (220 px, 1 px stroke at 10%), the helmet (a 400 × 330 box, 18 px from the top, centred; card 1's helmet faces right, card 2's faces left, so they face each other), seat line "CAR 9 · GER", name in Michroma 40 px on two lines, bio 16 px, three stats (24 ROUNDS, 1st F1 SEASON, nationality).
- **Helmets are the drivers' faces, in 3D.** `HermesHelmet` from the car package (`vendor/hermes-hm27`, README → `<HermesHelmet />`): one model, dressed from tokens. Driver 9 wears `gallery` (Gallery White shell, Carbon trim), driver 19 `graphite` (Graphite shell, Gallery White trim). Both: three Redline feathers just behind the visor (the mark's wings, longest on top, rising at 14°), a trim band along the bottom, rear and chin spoilers in the trim colour, the race number on both sides of the chin in Michroma (drawn at runtime from `content/drivers.ts`). No photos, no generated faces.
- **Still first, then live** (the specs section's pattern): the box shows the poster still (`public/images/drivers/helmet-*.webp`). When WebGL2 is there and data saver is off, `DriverHelmet` (package example) mounts over it and the still cross-fades out on `onReady` (200 ms). The live view renders exactly the still's frame (`HELMET_FRAME`), so nothing jumps. Live: a slow ±10° sway (one breath every 18 s), drag to turn (springs back). Reduced motion: no sway. Off screen: the canvas stops. Keep the still if anything fails.
- **The messenger** card under them (Carbon, red glow left): the same helmet in the `messenger` look, see-through with a Redline edge and glowing feathers, no shadow (his ghost car's look), still 360 px wide, live the same way; "TEST DRIVER · NAME WITHHELD", "THE MESSENGER", a line, his lap 1:11.906 in purple (44 px), and "Beat the messenger" (day) or "Opens at 06:00" (night).
- **Night (before Day One):** everything Graphite; both helmets in `beforeDawn` (dark shell, grey feathers, no number) in the night studio, with the red glow from behind; names replaced by two rounded bars; stats "—"; seat lines "CAR 1 · REVEALED AT 06:00"; the H1 and intro change (copy.md). Stills: `helmet-before-dawn-right` and `-left`.
