ABOUT HERMES MOTORS · BOILERPLATE

Hermes Motors is a new Grand Prix team that debuts in the 2027 season with the HM‑27, a car built to the 2026 rules: 1,006 horsepower, half of it electric, and active aerodynamics front and rear. The team revealed the car at 06:00 on Day One at its Proving Ground, where the public can race the test driver’s lap online. Speed is the message.

A note for editors: Hermes Motors is a fictional team, created as a design and development portfolio project. It is not affiliated with Formula 1, the FIA, Porsche or Hermès.
